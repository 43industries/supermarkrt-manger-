# ⚡ ETIMS Quick Start - EASIEST Method

## 🎯 The EASIEST Way (No Coding!)

**For most small businesses, the EASIEST way is manual submission.**

---

## ✅ 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Register (10 minutes)

1. Go to: **https://etims.kra.go.ke**
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Register"
3. Enter your KRA PIN
4. Complete registration
5. **Done!** ✅

---

### Step 2: Generate Invoice (Your System Already Does This!)

1. Login to your SureMartRess system
2. Go to **Finance → Invoices**
3. Create invoice from sale (as normal)
4. **Done!** ✅

**Your system already generates invoices - nothing new to learn!**

---

### Step 3: Submit to KRA (2-3 minutes)

1. Go to: **https://etims.kra.go.ke**
2. Login
3. Click "Submit Invoice"
4. Enter invoice details:
   - Invoice number
   - Date
   - Customer
   - Items
   - Amounts
5. Click "Submit"
6. **Done!** ✅

---

## 💡 Daily Routine

**Option 1: Submit After Each Sale**
- Complete sale → Generate invoice → Submit to KRA
- Time: 2-3 minutes per invoice

**Option 2: Submit at End of Day (Recommended)**
- Complete all sales → Generate all invoices → Submit all to KRA at once
- Time: 10-15 minutes at end of day
