# 🌐 How to Get Your Railway Domain

## 🎯 Find Your Live URL

### Step 1: Go to Railway Dashboard
1. Visit: https://railway.app
2. Log in
3. Click on your project (the one you deployed)

### Step 2: Find Your Domain
**Option A: From Project Dashboard**
- Look at the top of your project page
- You'll see your domain/URL displayed
- Example: `https://supermarkrt-manger-production.up.railway.app`

**Option B: From Deployments**
1. Click **"Deployments"** tab
2. Click on the latest (successful) deployment
3. Look for **"Domains"** or **"Service URL"**
4. Copy the URL

**Option C: From Settings**
1. Click **"Settings"** (in your project)
2. Go to **"Networking"** or **"Domains"** tab
3. You'll see your Railway domain there

---

## 📋 Domain Format

Railway domains look like:
```
https://your-project-name.up.railway.app
```

Or:
```
https://your-app-production.up.railway.app
```

Examples:
- `https://supermarkrt-manger-production.up.railway.app`
- `https://43-industries-pos.up.railway.app`
- `https://supermarket-pos-production.up.railway.app`

---

## ✅ After Deployment

Once your deployment is successful:

1. **Railway provides the URL automatically**
2. **Click the URL** to open your live site
3. **Share it** with others
4. **Bookmark it** for easy access

---

## 🔗 Your Live URL

**To find it:**
1. Railway Dashboard → Your Project
2. Look for the URL at the top
3. Or check: Settings → Domains

**It will look like:**
```
https://[your-project-name].up.railway.app
```

---

## 🌐 Custom Domain (Optional)

You can also add your own domain:

1. **Buy a domain** (Namecheap, GoDaddy, etc.)
2. **In Railway:**
   - Settings → Domains
   - Click **"Add Custom Domain"**
   - Enter your domain
   - Add DNS records as instructed
3. **SSL is automatic!** ✅

---

## 🎯 Quick Access

**To get your domain right now:**

1. **Go to:** https://railway.app/dashboard
2. **Click your project**
3. **Look at the top** - your URL is there!
4. **Copy it** and use it anywhere!
