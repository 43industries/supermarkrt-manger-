## 📋 What is ETIMS?

**ETIMS** (Electronic Tax Invoice Management System) is **mandatory in Kenya** since September 2023.

**Requirements:**
- All businesses must generate electronic tax invoices
- Invoices must be submitted to KRA (Kenya Revenue Authority)
- Real-time compliance required
