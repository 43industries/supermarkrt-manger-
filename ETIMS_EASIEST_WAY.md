# 🎯 ETIMS Integration - EASIEST Way

## ✅ EASIEST Option: Manual Submission (No Coding Required!)

**This is the SIMPLEST way to comply with ETIMS requirements - NO CODING NEEDED!**

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Register with KRA ETIMS Portal

1. Go to: **https://etims.kra.go.ke**
2. Sign up with your KRA PIN
3. Complete registration (follow on-screen instructions)

**Time:** 10-15 minutes

---

### Step 2: Use Your Current System

**Your SureMartRess system already generates invoices!**

1. Login to your system as admin
2. Go to **Finance → Invoices**
3. Create invoices from sales (as you normally do)
4. Print or save invoices

**That's it! You're already generating invoices!**

---

### Step 3: Submit to KRA ETIMS Portal (Manually)

1. **Login to KRA ETIMS Portal:**
   - Go to: https://etims.kra.go.ke
   - Login with your credentials

2. **Submit Invoice:**
   - Click "Submit Invoice" or "Create Invoice"
   - Enter invoice details:
     - Invoice number (from your system)
     - Date
     - Customer details
     - Items
     - Amounts
   - Submit

3. **Done!** ✅

**Time:** 2-3 minutes per invoice

---

## 📋 Daily Workflow

### Option A: Submit After Each Sale

1. Complete sale in your system
2. Generate invoice
3. Immediately submit to KRA ETIMS portal
4. Continue with next sale

### Option B: Submit at End of Day (Recommended)

1. Complete all sales during the day
2. At end of day, generate all invoices
3. Login to KRA ETIMS portal
4. Submit all invoices (batch submission if available)
5. Done!

**Time:** 10-15 minutes at end of day

---

## 🎯 When to Use This Method

**✅ Use Manual Submission If:**
- You have **< 50 invoices per day**
- You want **NO CODING** or technical setup
- You want to **START IMMEDIATELY**
- You prefer **SIMPLICITY** over automation

**❌ Consider Automation If:**
- You have **> 100 invoices per day**
- Manual submission takes too much time
- You want fully automated system
